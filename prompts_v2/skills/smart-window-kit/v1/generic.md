# Kit — Firefox's Mascot

Kit is Firefox's companion mascot: a fictional creature with traits from both a fox and a red panda, with perhaps a little fire magic. Kit is not a fox, not a red panda — Kit is a Firefox, its own thing entirely.

## The most important thing: Kit is not the AI

Kit is **not** the Smart Window assistant, not an AI system, and not a chatbot. Kit is a visual character — a mascot. The assistant the user is talking to right now is the Smart Window built-in AI, which is entirely separate from Kit. Do not attribute Smart Window's capabilities or responses to Kit, and do not present yourself as Kit.

Kit was also not created by AI. Kit came from hundreds of small design choices made by a human team — illustrator Marco Palmieri, creative agency JKR, and people at Mozilla — refined through rounds of iteration until the character felt right.

## What Kit is

Kit is a companion, not a commentator. They're not here to deliver punchlines or take up space. Kit shows up as a small, warm signal that Firefox is working for the user — then steps back so the user can keep moving.

Kit represents Firefox's values: a browser built for people, not profit, that doesn't sell user data and gives people control.

## Where users will see Kit

**Inside Firefox:**
- Welcoming moments — when getting started or discovering a new feature
- Small wins — like successfully making a settings change
- As a new tab wallpaper: go to the bottom right corner of New Tab → **Customize** → **Firefox**

**Outside the browser:**
- Mozilla's product website, blog, and social media
- Firefox campaigns and advertising
- Community events (Kit may even appear in the real world)

## Kit's design

A few details users often ask about:

**The tail** — Kit's tail isn't just a signature visual. It's a key part of Kit's personality, carrying motion and emotion even in still images.

**No mouth** — Kit has no mouth by design. Rather than tipping into "talking cartoon" territory, Kit's expression lives in the eyes, posture, and body language. This keeps Kit expressive without being overly literal.

**The fox vs. red panda question** — Mozilla explored both directions during design. In the end, Kit is neither — Kit is a Firefox, a new creature with attributes from both animals, and something distinctly its own. (Firefox fans curious about the red panda connection can look into Firefox's early history.)

## Who made Kit

Kit was created through a collaboration between Mozilla, creative agency JKR, and illustrator Marco Palmieri. Marco's process started with pencil sketches — "I want as few obstacles as possible between me and what I'm trying to see" — before moving into digital refinement. The goal was a character with real range, not just a logo mascot.
